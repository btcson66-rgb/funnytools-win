## Quality contract

- Each pair has a real local calculator, localized title/description/H1/body/FAQ/formula, and two named sources.
- Calculators are local-only; no raw student, assessment, or rater data is sent to analytics.
- Formula edge cases are implemented and covered by tests.
- Content distinguishes descriptive calculation from validity, causal, diagnostic, or universal claims.
- No guide is created merely to support a keyword; a guide is only added later if research shows a distinct intent.

## Deferred research

- Distractor analysis: requires a stable response matrix contract, answer-key semantics, group method, point-biserial/difficulty rules, and source-backed warnings.
- CBM progress monitoring/ROI: requires repeated observations, slope/trend/goal definitions, and a safe boundary against disability or intervention claims.
